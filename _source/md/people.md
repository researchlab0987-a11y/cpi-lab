#
Lab Director
[Dr. Tahsina Farah Sanam](https://sites.google.com/site/tahsinafarahsanam/home)
Dr. Tahsina Farah Sanam is a Professor in the Institute of Appropriate Technology at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology. Her principal research interests lie in the development of machine learning and statistical methodology, and computational system; especially for solving problems involving automated learning, reasoning, and decision-making in high-dimensional, multimodal, and dynamic possible worlds in artificial, healthcare, and social systems. Professor Sanam received a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Rutgers University, and M.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology. She serves as a guest editor of Frontiers in AI on special topics Fuzzy System Applications in Robotics and Automation. She is a member of IEEE, Society of Women Engineering (SWE-USA), and ACM.
