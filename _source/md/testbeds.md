[USRP Software Radio B210](http://www.ettus.com/product/details/UB210-KIT)

Network Interface Card
Intel WiFi Link 5300 is a family of IEEE 802.11a/b/g/Draft-N1 wireless network adapters that operate in both the 2.4 GHz and 5.0 GHz spectra. These adapters deliver up to 450 Mbps of bandwidth2 via features such as MIMO technology, 40 MHz Channel support, and Packet Aggregation.
